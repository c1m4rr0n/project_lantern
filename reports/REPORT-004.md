# Committee Report 004 — Self-service acquisition and onboarding
Date: 2026-09-18

## Why this was built now
The core workflow was usable only with a prewritten JSON company profile. That would force human setup and undermine the autonomy/low-support objective.

## Implemented
- Public landing page that states the narrow job-to-be-done and product boundary.
- Self-service company-profile onboarding.
- Immediate redirect into a freshly rescored opportunity feed after profile save.
- Validation pricing hypotheses: $39/month Solo and $119/month Team, with no payment collected yet.

## Pricing rationale
These are deliberately hypotheses rather than financial claims. Current competitors range from low-cost/self-service products through HigherGov's $500/year Starter and GovTribe's $1,900/year Launch Plus to sales-led suites such as GovDash. Lantern is testing whether a much narrower qualification workflow can convert without demos, onboarding calls or enterprise commitments.

## Human-work reduction
A tester can now go from landing page to customized ranked results without editing a file or asking the builder for setup.
