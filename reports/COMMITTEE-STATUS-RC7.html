WHY THE PIVOT

Do not defend sunk cost

Competitive review found substantial overlap in opportunity discovery, amendment monitoring and proposal/compliance workflows. Rather than keep adding similar features, Lantern reuses the same multi-tenant, scheduler, outbox, backup and SAM infrastructure for a narrower recurring job: tell me when one of my vendors appears in—or changes within—the official active exclusions data, and preserve what the screening saw.

UNIT ECONOMICS BY DESIGN

One upstream snapshot, local screening

RC7 does not query SAM.gov once per vendor. The daily Public V2 active-exclusions extract is downloaded and indexed once, then reused across all tenants. A vendor-only tenant also causes zero opportunity-feed calls. This keeps quota and marginal infrastructure cost low as watchlists grow.

LEGAL BOUNDARY

Monitoring signal, not a legal ruling

Current FAR provisions make active SAM exclusion records relevant to federal contracting and certain subcontracting workflows, but the effect depends on the exclusion basis and context. The UI therefore says ACTIVE EXCLUSION, shows official evidence and explicitly avoids claiming that Lantern itself has determined legal eligibility.
