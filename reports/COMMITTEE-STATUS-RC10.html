Hosted evidence

Domain, email and Stripe sandbox all exercised

exclusignal.com is registered and routed through Cloudflare/Railway. Resend produced a real HTTP 200 delivery. Stripe sandbox created Starter checkout, activated the tenant through signed webhooks, opened the Customer Portal and delivered the cancel-at-period-end update with HTTP 200.

No real card charge or MRR is claimed. The final customer.subscription.deleted event has not yet occurred.

What the real journey exposed

Five issues that local tests did not surface clearly

Verification email latency, generic onboarding errors, old DEMO opportunities visible in a live-SAM tenant, internal RC labels, and a missing favicon. A separate Stripe 400 pattern was traced to an accidentally duplicated webhook destination with a different signing secret; deleting the duplicate removed new 400 deliveries.

RC10 corrections

Vendor Watch becomes the actual first-run experience

Verification/login/reset now route to Vendor Watch. Pursuit profile is optional. Auth email attempts immediate Resend delivery but retains the durable outbox for retry. Live SAM mode filters/purges internal demo fixtures. Profile errors are actionable. Public UI shows Beta rather than internal release identifiers, includes a favicon, and shows scheduled cancellation dates.

Cost reality

APIs remain $0 mandatory; cash spend is no longer zero

The confirmed cash purchase is $10.46 for one year of the domain. Railway invoice/usage has not been audited as a final charge, so no additional hosting spend is claimed. Stripe sandbox charges are fake test transactions. Resend is currently within its free allowance.

Remaining gates

Do not call this a finished paid SaaS yet

Stripe live onboarding, tax/legal/privacy decisions, formal trademark review, production monitoring, independent disaster recovery, durable source deployment and a real external customer's willingness to pay remain. Horizontal scale still requires moving beyond single-instance SQLite.
