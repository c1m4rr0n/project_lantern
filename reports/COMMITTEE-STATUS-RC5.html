Change Watch

RC5 moves Lantern beyond “find and score opportunities.” Once a contractor chooses to review or pursue a notice, Lantern keeps that work item alive outside the rolling discovery window and records material public changes from SAM.gov with an auditable before → after trail.

What changed

  • Tracked work no longer disappears. Reviewing/pursue opportunities survive the short discovery lookback.
  • Direct official refresh. Lantern refreshes actively tracked SAM notices by notice ID with a cached, bounded query.
  • Material change history. Deadline, title, solicitation/type, agency, set-aside, PSC, NAICS, active status, place of performance and resource links are fingerprinted and diffed.
  • User acknowledgement. Changes remain visible until reviewed; they are not silently overwritten.
  • Digest integration. Morning briefs can surface tracked pursuits that changed.

Why this matters commercially

Competitive review showed that “tell me which bid to pursue” is already offered by other procurement-intelligence products. RC5 narrows Lantern toward a more defensible evidence-first workflow: qualify a notice, retain the pursuit, preserve official evidence and warn when material public facts change. It does not claim to replace legal review of amendments.
