## Service description

ExcluSignal offers Vendor Exclusion Watch: customer-managed vendor identifiers, screening against public SAM.gov exclusions, evidence/history, change notifications, CSV import and reports. Pursuit Watch is secondary and provides deterministic matching, change tracking and requirement candidates. Human verification is necessary. ExcluSignal does not make legal eligibility determinations; exact identifiers support identity matching, name-only matches require review, and no match is limited to the referenced snapshot.

## Account and access model

Email verification and password authentication are required. Each account currently represents one tenant; there are no multi-user roles or invitations. Scale is a larger-capacity plan, not a collaboration entitlement. Customers should supply only information they are authorized to use, protect downloaded reports, and avoid CUI/export-controlled information and secrets.

## Billing behavior for review

Current product configuration provides a 14-day/25-vendor trial, Starter at a proposed $39/month for 50 active vendors and Scale at a proposed $99/month for 500. Pricing remains a validation hypothesis; approved offers, taxes, refunds, renewals, notice requirements and cancellation terms require business/legal review. Stripe hosts checkout and subscription management. Inactive entitlement makes protected mutations/screening read-only while history remains accessible. Archived vendors do not count as active slots; restoring requires capacity.

No Stripe Live activation or real charge is part of RC17–RC19. Account deletion is not a substitute for cancellation: deletion is blocked while a subscription is active or its closure cannot be confirmed. A scheduled cancellation remains active until it actually ends.

## Data and operational boundaries

Customers can export their tenant data after password confirmation. Deletion removes current tenant data and credentials through a recoverable workflow; backups and providers have separate retention. Public-source availability, snapshot freshness, notification delivery and service availability have technical limits. Service levels, warranties, liability, acceptable use, IP permissions and dispute terms remain unresolved and must not be inferred from this draft.
